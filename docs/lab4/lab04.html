Stone & Flesh

Play

Game Story

For centuries, you have slept soundly as the ancient guardian of a hidden subterranean sanctuary. But your peaceful slumber is abruptly shattered when a disgusting plague breaks out in the world above, sending hordes of mindless, rotting undead tumbling down into your home.

Awakened from your stone sleep by the sudden intrusion, you find your once-pristine domain infested with brainless intruders, foul stench, and green slime. Driven by pure guardian instinct (and an absolute refusal to let zombies ruin your quiet dungeon), you must raise your massive stone fists and smash, crush, and clear out every last piece of decaying flesh to reclaim your home!

Game Style

Stone & Flesh is a fast-paced 2D action platformer featuring vibrant pixel art with expressive cartoon animations, combining precise jumping across tricky dungeon hazards with heavy, satisfying melee combat to crush hordes of zombies.
